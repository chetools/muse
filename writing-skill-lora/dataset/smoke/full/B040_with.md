On-time delivery over the last two quarters was 85% against a target of 95%, on order records for 12 months covering 13779 orders at a contract packaging firm running 3 plants with revenue of $106 million per year. Plant 2 accounts for 57% of late orders. Overtime cost was $1.7 million last year, up 21% on the year before, and the order backlog stands at 3.6 weeks against 1.6 weeks a year ago. A scheduling system for all plants is quoted at $295k; Plant 2 alone is $139k. An internal estimate (not a vendor figure) puts the overtime reduction at Plant 2 at 15-20% in the first year. The Plant 2 installation ($139k against $295k for all plants) is the step the evidence supports first: the plant carrying 57% of late orders is treated first, and the remaining plants wait on its reported results. The scheduling programme is the next step, and the operations director owns it.
 The figures behind the recommendation are stated on the same evidence base throughout, order records for 12 months and 13779 orders, and the held plants are held on that same base until Plant 2 reports.
