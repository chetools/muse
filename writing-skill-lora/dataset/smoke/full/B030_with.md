A metered trial on one of the three refrigeration lines, owned by the plant engineer, is recommended before any capital decision on the heat-recovery retrofit. Refrigeration at the food plant uses 2362 MWh of electricity per year. The equipment vendor projects a saving of 12% of that use; at the vendor figure, the derived saving is 283 MWh per year. Capital cost is $367k. At an electricity price of $0.12 per kWh, the derived annual saving is $34k and the derived simple payback is 10.8 years. A payback of 10.8 years rests on the vendor projection: the saving was not measured at this plant. Refrigeration load in the summer peak month was not modelled, and the projected saving carries no measurement from a peak month. The trial replaces the projection with a measurement on one line before capital is committed. One line is metered, the saving is measured on that line, and the capital decision is held until the trial result is reported. The vendor figure (12%, 283 MWh per year derived) stays labelled as a projection in every use, and the derived money figures ($34k per year, 10.8 years) stay tied to the electricity price stated with them ($0.12 per kWh). The trial is the next step, and the plant engineer owns it.
 The recommendation holds the capital decision behind the trial on every point: no capital decision is taken on the vendor projection alone, and the decision follows the metered result from the one line placed on trial. Refrigeration use (2362 MWh per year) is the base to which the projected 12% applies, and the trial measures the saving against that same refrigeration duty on the line under test.
