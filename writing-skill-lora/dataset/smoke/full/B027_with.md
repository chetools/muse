Adsorption of a dye from water on a metal-organic framework gave a fitted Langmuir capacity of 148 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6, in batch tests at 298 K, and removal of 83% of the initial dye concentration after 120 min at an adsorbent dose of 0.8 g L-1. Capacity and removal are stated on their own bases. The Langmuir capacity (148 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent) is a fitted value, from the Langmuir fit to the batch measurements at 298 K, and the fit is reported for pH 6. Removal is a measured outcome at a stated dose and contact time: at an adsorbent dose of 0.8 g L-1, removal after 120 min was 83% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1. The dose fixes the mass of adsorbent behind the removal figure, and the initial concentration fixes the amount of dye the percentage refers to. A removal figure at another dose, or against another initial concentration, is a different measurement and is not reported. The capacity figure is bounded by its pH. The Langmuir fit is reported for pH 6 only, and capacity at other pH values was not measured; the value 148 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent is the capacity at pH 6, at 298 K, and no capacity at another pH is stated or implied by the fit. The result stands at its tested scope: a fitted Langmuir capacity of 148 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6 and 298 K, and 83% removal after 120 min at a dose of 0.8 g L-1 from an initial concentration of 50 mg L-1, with capacity at other pH values unmeasured. Removal and capacity are not interchangeable figures: removal is stated at a dose and a time, capacity as a fitted value at a pH.
