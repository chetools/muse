This executive summary looks at delivery performance at a contract packaging firm that runs 2 plants and has revenue of $120 million per year. The analysis is based on order records for 12 months, covering 15666 orders in total, so it rests on a fairly complete picture of recent operations.

On-time delivery over the last two quarters was 87%, which is below the target of 95% that the firm is aiming for. Overtime cost $2.1 million last year, which was up 16% on the year before, and the order backlog now stands at 3.7 weeks, compared with 1.8 weeks a year ago, so the situation appears to be getting worse on several measures at the same time.

Plant 2 is clearly at the centre of the problem, since it accounts for 68% of late orders. A scheduling system for all plants is quoted at $300k, while Plant 2 alone would cost $126k. An internal estimate puts the overtime reduction at Plant 2 at 15-20% in the first year, and this is an internal estimate rather than a vendor figure.

The recommendation under consideration is therefore to install the system at Plant 2 first, and to hold the other plants until the Plant 2 results have been reported. The operations director owns the scheduling programme.
