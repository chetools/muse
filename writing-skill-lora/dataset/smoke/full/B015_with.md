A first-order liquid-phase reaction gave a rate constant k (s-1) of 0.042 s-1 at 300 K and a derived 1.444 s-1 at 340 K. Rate constant k at 300 K was 0.042 s-1, measured directly. Rate constant k at 340 K was 1.444 s-1, computed from the Arrhenius relation with activation energy 75 kJ mol-1, and the 340 K value is a derived value rather than a direct measurement. Each temperature was measured in triplicate. Relative standard deviation of k was 4% across the triplicate measurements at each temperature. Sampling sets the upper limit of the reported range: above 340 K the solvent evaporated during sampling, and no rate constant is reported above 340 K. Temperature dependence in the measured range is steep, with k at 340 K (1.444 s-1, derived) standing at about thirty-four times k at 300 K (0.042 s-1, measured). Precision of the underlying measurements is set by the triplicate design and the relative standard deviation of 4%.
