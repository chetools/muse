Recommendation under consideration: install the scheduling system at Plant 2 first, at a quoted $126k, and hold the other plants until the Plant 2 results are reported. The operations director owns the scheduling programme.

A contract packaging firm runs 2 plants; revenue is $120 million per year. Order records for 12 months, covering 15666 orders, are the evidence base. On-time delivery over the last two quarters was 87%, against a target of 95%. Overtime cost was $2.1 million last year, up 16% on the year before. The order backlog stands at 3.7 weeks, against 1.8 weeks a year ago. Delivery, overtime and backlog move together in the records: on-time delivery below target, overtime above the year before, and a backlog above its level of a year ago.

Plant 2 accounts for 68% of late orders. A scheduling system for all plants is quoted at $300k; Plant 2 alone is $126k. An internal estimate, not a vendor figure, puts the overtime reduction at Plant 2 at 15-20% in the first year. Installation at Plant 2 first holds the remaining plants at no stated cost until the Plant 2 results are reported, and the quoted figures keep the two stages separate: $126k for Plant 2, $300k for all plants.
