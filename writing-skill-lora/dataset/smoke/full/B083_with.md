Recommendation: run a three-month pilot of maintenance sensors on 24 pumps, at a cost of $35k, before any decision on full installation. The reliability engineering group owns the pilot and reports before the March capital review. The plant runs 260 pumps, and the question is whether sensors should be fitted across all 260 or tested first on a small set.

Maintenance records for 12 months show 965 h of unplanned downtime, with bearing failures at 41% of the downtime hours. A sensor set for all 260 pumps is quoted at $433k installed, plus $76k per year. In a vendor trial on a comparable unit, the sensors cut downtime by 24%; the figure is a vendor figure and was not measured at this plant. Spare-part lead times are unchanged by the sensors.
