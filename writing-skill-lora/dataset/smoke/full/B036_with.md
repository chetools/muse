An amine absorption unit at pilot scale captured 87% of the carbon dioxide in treated cement kiln gas over 60 days, at a steam demand of 3.1 gigajoules per tonne captured. The unit treated gas at 14 t of carbon dioxide per day. Steam demand was 3.1 gigajoules per tonne of carbon dioxide captured, and solvent loss was 1.5 kg per tonne captured. Capture, steam demand and solvent loss are reported for the same unit over the same period: 87% capture at 14 t per day, held over 60 days, with the steam demand and the solvent loss each stated per tonne of carbon dioxide captured. Beyond 60 days, the effect of kiln dust on the solvent is untested, and the reported figures (87%, 3.1 gigajoules per tonne, 1.5 kg per tonne) cover the 60 days tested and no longer period. The pilot result is a 60-day result at the stated rate, and any statement about solvent behaviour with kiln dust beyond that period has no test behind it in this work. Over the 60 days at 14 t of carbon dioxide per day, the pilot unit captured 87% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas, at 3.1 gigajoules of steam and 1.5 kg of solvent loss per tonne captured.
 The three figures belong to one test and are stated on one basis throughout, per tonne of carbon dioxide captured for steam and solvent, and as a share of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas for capture.
