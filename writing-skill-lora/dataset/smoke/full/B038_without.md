This study measured the adsorption of a dye from water on a metal-organic framework in batch tests at 298 K. The data were fitted with the Langmuir model, and the fitted Langmuir capacity was 186 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6. In the batch tests, an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1 was used. At that dose, the removal after 120 min was 91% of the initial dye concentration, which was 50 mg L-1. So, after 120 min, 91% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1 had been removed at a dose of 0.6 g L-1. It should be noted that the Langmuir fit is reported for pH 6 only. The capacity at other pH values was not measured, so the fitted capacity of 186 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent applies to pH 6 and not to other pH values. In summary, the batch tests at 298 K gave a fitted Langmuir capacity of 186 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6, and a removal of 91% after 120 min at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1 from an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1. These are the results reported for the adsorption of the dye on the metal-organic framework under the conditions tested.
