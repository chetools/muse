This example calculates the Reynolds number for water in a pipe and states the flow regime: Re is 44900, and the flow is turbulent. The Reynolds number Re (dimensionless) is computed from density rho (kg m-3), mean velocity v (m s-1), pipe diameter D (m) and dynamic viscosity mu (Pa s) by Re = rho v D / mu.

Water at 293 K has density rho of 998 kg m-3 and dynamic viscosity mu of 1.00 x 10-3 Pa s. Pipe diameter D is 0.025 m and mean velocity v is 1.8 m s-1. Substitution of the four values into the Reynolds number relation thus gives Re = 998 x 1.8 x 0.025 / 0.001, and the calculation gives Re = 44900.

Flow is laminar below Re = 2300 and turbulent above Re = 4000; between the two values the regime is transitional and is not assigned by the number alone. Comparison of the computed value with the two thresholds shows that 44900 stands above 4000. The regime statement rests on that comparison alone, at the stated inputs.
