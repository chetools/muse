Batch tests at 298 K gave activated carbon a fitted Langmuir capacity of 248 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 7, with removal reaching 88% of an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1 after 120 min at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1. Langmuir capacity (the fitted capacity in mg of dye per gram of adsorbent) was 248 mg g-1 at pH 7. Removal performance was measured at the same temperature: at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1, 88% of the initial dye concentration (50 mg L-1) was removed after 120 min. Batch conditions were held at 298 K across the tests. Reported capacity rests on the Langmuir fit at pH 7 only. Capacity at other pH values was not measured, and no capacity figure is reported for any pH other than 7. Removal is reported for one set of conditions: an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1, an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1, and a contact time of 120 min, at 298 K. Dye removal on activated carbon at 298 K is characterised at pH 7 by a fitted Langmuir capacity of 248 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent and by 88% removal after 120 min at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1 from an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1, with capacity at other pH values unmeasured. Contact time in the removal test was 120 min, and the dose in that test was 0.6 g L-1. Temperature in both the capacity fit and the removal test was 298 K. Conditions beyond the reported set, including capacity at other pH values, were not measured.
