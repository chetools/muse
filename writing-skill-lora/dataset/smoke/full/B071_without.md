Adsorption of a dye from water was studied using a zeolite 13X sample, with the experiments carried out in batch tests at a temperature of 298 K. The results were analysed using the Langmuir model, and the fitted Langmuir capacity was found to be 232 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent. This fitted capacity applies at pH 7, which was the pH condition used for the reported fit.

Alongside the capacity measurement, the removal performance was also assessed. At an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1, the removal after 120 min reached 85% of the initial dye concentration, which was 50 mg L-1. This shows the level of dye removal achieved under the specific batch conditions employed in the tests, namely a temperature of 298 K, an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1, and a contact time of 120 min, starting from an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1.

It is important to be clear about the scope of these findings. The Langmuir fit is reported for pH 7 only, and the capacity at other pH values was not measured. Consequently, the fitted capacity of 232 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent should not be assumed to apply at other pH values on the basis of this work.

In summary, in batch tests at 298 K, adsorption of a dye from water on a zeolite 13X sample gave a fitted Langmuir capacity of 232 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 7, and at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1 the removal after 120 min was 85% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1. The capacity figure rests on the Langmuir fit at pH 7 only, since capacity at other pH values was not measured in these batch tests at 298 K using a zeolite 13X sample and an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1 over 120 min.
