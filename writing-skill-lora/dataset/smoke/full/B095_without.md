This executive summary presents an overview of delivery performance at a contract packaging firm that operates 4 plants and has revenue of $112 million per year. The analysis is based on order records covering a period of 12 months, comprising a total of 16892 orders. Over the last two quarters, on-time delivery has been running at 90%, which is below the target level of 95% that the firm has set for itself. Overtime cost was $2.4 million last year, which was up 29% on the year before, and the order backlog currently stands at 4.1 weeks, compared with 2 weeks a year ago, indicating that the delivery problem has been getting worse rather than better over the period covered by the records.

A closer look at the data shows that Plant 2 accounts for 67% of all late orders, which makes it the clear focus of the problem. A new scheduling system has been quoted at $317k for all plants, or at $126k for Plant 2 alone. An internal estimate suggests that installing the system at Plant 2 could reduce overtime there by 15-20% in the first year. The recommendation under consideration is therefore to install the system at Plant 2 first, and to hold the other plants until the results from Plant 2 have been reported. The operations director owns the scheduling programme.
