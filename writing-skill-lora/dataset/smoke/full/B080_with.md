An amine absorption unit was tested at pilot scale on gas from a cement kiln. The unit captured 88% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas over 90 days at 11 t of carbon dioxide per day. Steam demand was 3.1 gigajoules per tonne of carbon dioxide captured. Solvent loss was 2 kg per tonne of carbon dioxide captured.

Capture, steam demand and solvent loss stand on the same basis: the 90 days of operation, at 11 t of carbon dioxide per day, on gas from a cement kiln. Steam demand and solvent loss are each stated per tonne captured, at the capture level recorded over the same period. The unit ran at pilot scale for the full 90 days, and the reported values cover that whole period at the stated daily rate.

Beyond 90 days, the effect of kiln dust on the solvent is untested. Solvent behaviour past 90 days is not reported, and no value for capture, steam demand or solvent loss beyond the 90 days is given. At 90 days and 11 t of carbon dioxide per day, the unit holds a capture level of 88%, a steam demand of 3.1 gigajoules per tonne captured and a solvent loss of 2 kg per tonne captured.
