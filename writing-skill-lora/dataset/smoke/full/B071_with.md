Adsorption of a dye from water on a zeolite 13X sample was measured in batch tests at 298 K. The fitted Langmuir capacity was 232 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 7. At an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1, removal after 120 min was 85% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1. Capacity and removal were recorded in the same batch tests at 298 K: a fitted Langmuir capacity of 232 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 7, and removal of 85% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1 after 120 min at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1.

The Langmuir fit is reported for pH 7 only; capacity at other pH values was not measured. The fitted Langmuir capacity of 232 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent is limited to pH 7, and the removal value of 85% is limited to the stated batch conditions: 298 K, an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1, an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1, and 120 min. Removal after 120 min (85% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1 at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1) and the fitted Langmuir capacity at pH 7 (232 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent) stand as the two reported results at 298 K.
