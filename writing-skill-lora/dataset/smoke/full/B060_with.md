In batch tests at 298 K, a metal-organic framework took up dye from water with a fitted Langmuir capacity of 197 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6, and at an adsorbent dose of 0.9 g L-1 removal after 120 min was 87% of the initial dye concentration (50 mg L-1). Adsorption was measured in batch tests, one temperature (298 K) throughout, and the two reported quantities describe different aspects of the same tests: the Langmuir capacity is a fitted parameter of the model at pH 6, and the removal figure is a measured share of the initial concentration removed at one stated dose and one stated contact time.

Removal depends on the dose and the contact time at which it is read. At a dose of 0.9 g L-1, removal after 120 min reached 87% of the initial dye concentration (50 mg L-1); removal at other doses, or at contact times shorter or longer than 120 min, is not reported, and the removal figure cannot be separated from the dose and time bundled with it. The fitted Langmuir capacity (197 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent) stands on the fit at pH 6, and the fit is reported for pH 6 only.

Capacity at other pH values was not measured. No Langmuir fit is reported at any pH other than 6, so the capacity of the metal-organic framework under more acidic or more alkaline conditions is unstated in this work, and the fitted value (197 mg per gram) is a statement about pH 6 at 298 K. Both quantities, the fitted capacity and the removal at the stated dose and time, are bounded by the conditions of the batch tests: one adsorbent, one initial dye concentration (50 mg L-1), one temperature (298 K), and, for the capacity, one pH (6). Within those bounds, the framework removed 87% of the initial dye in 120 min at a dose of 0.9 g L-1, against a fitted Langmuir capacity of 197 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6.
