Recommendation: run a metered trial of the heat-recovery retrofit on one of the three refrigeration lines before any capital decision. The plant uses 2364 MWh of electricity per year in refrigeration. The equipment vendor projects a saving of 16% of that use, a derived saving of 378 MWh per year at the vendor figure; at a capital cost of $409k and an electricity price of $0.12 per kWh, the derived annual saving is $45k and the derived simple payback is 9.1 years. Every saving figure in this summary is derived from the vendor projection and is labelled as derived for that reason.

The saving figure is a vendor projection and was not measured at this plant. Refrigeration load in the summer peak month was not modelled, so the projection's coverage of the year's load is incomplete at the peak. The payback (9.1 years) inherits both limits: it is computed from a projected saving, at a stated price ($0.12 per kWh), against the full capital cost ($409k), with the peak month absent from the model behind the saving.

The plant engineer owns the trial. A metered trial on one of the three lines replaces the projected saving for that line with a measured one, and the capital decision is held until the trial is reported.
