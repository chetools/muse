Over 45 days at 9 t of carbon dioxide per day, a pilot amine absorption unit on cement kiln gas captured 92% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas, at a steam demand of 3.3 gigajoules per tonne captured and a solvent loss of 2.1 kg per tonne captured. Capture is reported for the treated gas over the full test period: 92% of the carbon dioxide in that gas, at 9 t of carbon dioxide per day. Steam demand (3.3 gigajoules per tonne of carbon dioxide captured) and solvent loss (2.1 kg per tonne captured) are reported on the same per-tonne basis. Test duration was 45 days at pilot scale. Kiln dust is the named gap in the test programme: beyond 45 days, the effect of kiln dust on the solvent is untested. Reported performance therefore covers 45 days of operation, and solvent behaviour under kiln dust exposure beyond 45 days stands outside the reported results. Capture performance at pilot scale on cement kiln gas was 92% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas over 45 days at 9 t per day, with steam demand of 3.3 gigajoules per tonne captured and solvent loss of 2.1 kg per tonne captured, and the effect of kiln dust on the solvent beyond 45 days untested. Operating rate throughout the test was 9 t of carbon dioxide per day, on gas from a cement kiln, at pilot scale.
