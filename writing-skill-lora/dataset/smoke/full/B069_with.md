An amine absorption unit tested at pilot scale on gas from a cement kiln captured 84% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas over 45 days at 10 t of carbon dioxide per day. Steam demand was 3.8 gigajoules per tonne of carbon dioxide captured, and solvent loss was 1.5 kg per tonne captured. Capture, steam demand and solvent loss were recorded over the same 45 days at the same rate (10 t of carbon dioxide per day): 84% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas, 3.8 gigajoules per tonne captured, and 1.5 kg per tonne captured.
