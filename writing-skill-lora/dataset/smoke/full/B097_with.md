Calculate the Reynolds number for water in a pipe, and state the flow regime. The result is Re = 79800 (dimensionless), and the flow is turbulent.

The Reynolds number Re is computed from density rho (kg m-3), mean velocity v (m s-1), pipe diameter D (m) and dynamic viscosity mu (Pa s) by Re = rho v D / mu. Water at 293 K has rho = 998 kg m-3 and mu = 1.00 x 10-3 Pa s. Pipe diameter is D = 0.08 m, and mean velocity is v = 1 m s-1. Each input belongs to the same flow: one fluid, at one temperature, in one pipe, at one mean velocity.

Substitution of the stated values gives Re = 998 x 1 x 0.08 / 0.001. Thus Re = 79800, from the density, velocity and diameter in the numerator and the viscosity in the denominator of the stated relation. A change in any one input changes the result through that relation: diameter and velocity enter in proportion, and viscosity enters in inverse proportion, at the stated values of the remaining inputs.

Flow is laminar below Re = 2300 and turbulent above Re = 4000; between the two values the regime is transitional and is not assigned by the number alone. Comparison of the computed value with the two thresholds places 79800 above the turbulent threshold, by a wide margin over 4000 and far from the transitional band. Water in a pipe of diameter 0.08 m at a mean velocity of 1 m s-1 and 293 K is in turbulent flow, at Re = 79800.
