Adsorption of a dye from water on a metal-organic framework gave a fitted Langmuir capacity of 186 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6, in batch tests at 298 K. Removal after 120 min was 91% of the initial dye concentration (50 mg L-1) at an adsorbent dose of 0.6 g L-1.
