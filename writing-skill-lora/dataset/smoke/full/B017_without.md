This memo concerns the possible installation of maintenance sensors on the pumps at the plant, where a total of 214 pumps are currently in operation. According to the maintenance records covering a period of 12 months, the plant experienced 1352 h of unplanned downtime, which is a considerable amount of lost production time. Bearing failures are a major contributor to this problem, accounting for 52% of the downtime hours recorded. A sensor set covering all 214 pumps has been quoted at $462k installed, together with an ongoing cost of $69k per year. In a trial conducted by the vendor on a comparable unit, the sensors were reported to cut downtime by 20%; however, this figure is a vendor figure, and it was not measured at this plant. A more limited pilot, covering 18 pumps, would cost $34k over a period of three months. It should also be noted that spare-part lead times are unchanged by the sensors. This memo therefore recommends the three-month pilot. The reliability engineering group owns the pilot and will report before the March capital review, and the decision on full installation will be held until the pilot results have been reported. The pilot covers 18 of the 214 pumps at the plant, over the three-month pilot period recommended in this memo.
