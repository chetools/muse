Adsorption of a dye from water on activated carbon was measured in batch tests at 298 K. The fitted Langmuir capacity was 202 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 7. At an adsorbent dose of 0.5 g L-1, removal after 120 min was 94% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1. Each figure stands with its conditions in its sentence: the capacity with the fit and the pH, the removal with the dose, the time and the initial concentration.

The Langmuir capacity is a fitted figure, from the Langmuir fit to the batch data at 298 K, and the fit is reported for pH 7. Removal is a measured batch result at the stated dose: 94% of the initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1, removed after 120 min at an adsorbent dose of 0.5 g L-1. Capacity and removal describe different quantities on different bases, one per gram of adsorbent from a fit, one as a percentage of the initial concentration in a single batch test, and neither figure substitutes for the other.

Restatement of the batch conditions fixes what each figure covers. Batch tests at 298 K, at pH 7 for the fit, at a dose of 0.5 g L-1 and an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1 for the removal test, with removal read after 120 min: the conditions are the whole of the reported case, and no capacity or removal at another temperature, dose or initial concentration is reported.

The Langmuir fit is reported for pH 7 only; capacity at other pH values was not measured. The fitted capacity of 202 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent and the removal of 94% after 120 min are limited to pH 7 and to the stated batch conditions at 298 K.
