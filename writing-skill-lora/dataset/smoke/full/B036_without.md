An amine absorption unit was tested at pilot scale on gas from a cement kiln, and this passage reports how the unit performed during the test period. Over 60 days, at a rate of 14 t of carbon dioxide per day, the unit captured 87% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas. The steam demand for the unit was 3.1 gigajoules per tonne of carbon dioxide captured. The solvent loss was 1.5 kg per tonne captured. These figures describe the performance of the pilot unit over the 60 days of testing on the cement kiln gas. It is important to note that, beyond 60 days, the effect of kiln dust on the solvent is untested. This means that the results reported here cover the 60-day test period, and the longer-term behaviour of the solvent in the presence of kiln dust is not covered by this work. In summary, the pilot unit treated gas from a cement kiln at 14 t of carbon dioxide per day over 60 days, it captured 87% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas, its steam demand was 3.1 gigajoules per tonne of carbon dioxide captured, and its solvent loss was 1.5 kg per tonne captured. The passage therefore reports capture, steam demand and solvent loss together for the same pilot test, over the same 60 days and at the same rate of 14 t of carbon dioxide per day.
