This executive summary reviews delivery performance at a contract packaging firm that runs 3 plants and has revenue of $106 million per year. The evidence base is order records for 12 months, covering 13779 orders. On-time delivery over the last two quarters was 85%, against a target of 95%. Overtime cost was $1.7 million last year, which is up 21% on the year before. The order backlog stands at 3.6 weeks, compared with 1.6 weeks a year ago. Plant 2 accounts for 57% of late orders, so a large share of the late orders comes from that one plant. A scheduling system for all plants is quoted at $295k, while Plant 2 alone is $139k. An internal estimate puts the overtime reduction at Plant 2 at 15-20% in the first year; this is an internal estimate and not a vendor figure. The recommendation under consideration is to install at Plant 2 first and hold the other plants until the Plant 2 results are reported. The operations director owns the scheduling programme. Overall, the picture is that delivery performance is below target, overtime and backlog have both risen, and the proposal is to start with Plant 2 rather than installing the system at all plants at once.
