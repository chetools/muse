Run a metered trial on one of the three refrigeration lines before any capital decision on the heat-recovery retrofit. Refrigeration at the food plant uses 1893 MWh of electricity per year, and the case for the retrofit rests on a vendor projection: a saving of 17% of that use, a derived saving of 322 MWh per year at the vendor figure. Capital cost is $312k, and at an electricity price of $0.13 per kWh the derived annual saving is $42k, with a derived simple payback of 7.4 years. Each of those figures is derived from the vendor projection, and the saving was not measured at this plant. Refrigeration load in the summer peak month was not modelled, and the annual figures carry no measurement from a peak month. A metered trial on one line measures the saving at this plant before $312k is committed. The trial replaces a projected saving with a measured saving on one of the three lines. The plant engineer owns the trial. The capital decision is held until the trial is reported, and the reported saving, not the vendor projection, is the figure the decision rests on. The trial runs on one of the three lines, and the metered saving on that line is reported before the capital decision is taken.
