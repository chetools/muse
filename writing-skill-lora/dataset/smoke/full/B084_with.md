Recommendation under consideration: install the scheduling system at Plant 2 first, at $138k, and hold the other plants until Plant 2 results are reported. The operations director owns the scheduling programme. The firm runs 4 plants with revenue of $79 million per year, on-time delivery stands below target, and late orders are concentrated at Plant 2.

The evidence base is order records for 12 months, 14326 orders. On-time delivery over the last two quarters was 84%, against a target of 95%. Overtime cost was $1.8 million last year, up 21% on the year before. The order backlog stands at 3.4 weeks, against 1.9 weeks a year ago. Plant 2 accounts for 62% of late orders.

A scheduling system for all plants is quoted at $319k; Plant 2 alone is $138k. An internal estimate, not a vendor figure, puts the overtime reduction at Plant 2 at 15-20% in the first year. Installation at the remaining plants is held until Plant 2 results are reported, and the decision on those plants rests on the Plant 2 results.
