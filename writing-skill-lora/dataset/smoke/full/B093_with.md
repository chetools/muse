At 298 K in batch tests, a metal-organic framework adsorbed dye from water with a fitted Langmuir capacity of 185 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 7. Removal after 120 min was 85% of the initial dye concentration (50 mg L-1) at an adsorbent dose of 1.1 g L-1. Capacity and removal are reported for the same test series, at one temperature (298 K) and one pH (pH 7). Batch tests at 298 K are the setting for both figures, and the reported capacity and the reported removal both belong to that setting and to no other.

Langmuir capacity is a fitted quantity: 185 mg per gram comes from the Langmuir fit to the batch data, at pH 7. Removal is a measured outcome at a stated dose and contact time, 85% after 120 min at 1.1 g L-1 from an initial concentration of 50 mg L-1. Dose, contact time and initial concentration fix the removal figure; a different dose or a different contact time is a different test, outside the reported set.

The Langmuir fit is reported for pH 7 only; capacity at other pH values was not measured. At pH 7 and 298 K, the fitted capacity is 185 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent, and removal at a dose of 1.1 g L-1 reached 85% of the initial dye concentration after 120 min. Capacity at any other pH is outside the measured record.
