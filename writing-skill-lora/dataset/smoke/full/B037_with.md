The rate constant k of a first-order liquid-phase reaction was 0.021 s-1 at 300 K and, as a derived value, 1.398 s-1 at 340 K. The rate constant k (s-1) was measured at two temperatures. At 300 K, k = 0.021 s-1. At 340 K, k = 1.398 s-1, computed from the Arrhenius relation with activation energy 89 kJ mol-1 as a derived value. Each temperature was measured in triplicate; the relative standard deviation of k was 4%.
