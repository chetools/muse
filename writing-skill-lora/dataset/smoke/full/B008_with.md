Run a metered trial of the heat-recovery retrofit on one of the three refrigeration lines before any capital decision; the vendor projection behind the full retrofit gives a derived simple payback of 22.3 years on a capital cost of $469k. Refrigeration at the food plant uses 1871 MWh of electricity per year. Heat recovery is projected by the equipment vendor to save 11% of that use. Saving at the vendor figure is a derived 206 MWh per year. Annual saving at an electricity price of $0.1 per kWh is a derived $21k. Simple payback on the $469k capital cost is a derived 22.3 years. Status of the saving figure matters to the decision: the 11% saving is a vendor projection, and the saving was not measured at this plant. Refrigeration load in the summer peak month was not modelled, and no measured saving at summer peak load stands in the evidence. Trade-off in one line: a metered trial on one of three lines costs the trial only, and the $469k capital decision waits for a measured saving in place of a vendor projection with a derived payback of 22.3 years. Trial ownership sits with the plant engineer, who owns the trial. The metered trial is on one refrigeration line; the other two lines continue without the retrofit while the trial runs. Measured trial results will replace the vendor projection (11% of 1871 MWh per year) as the basis for the capital decision.
