Adsorption of a dye from water on a metal-organic framework was measured in batch tests at 298 K. The data were fitted with the Langmuir model, and the fitted Langmuir capacity was 163 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent at pH 6. This capacity describes how much dye the material can take up under the conditions of the fit.

In a separate batch test at an adsorbent dose of 0.7 g L-1, removal after 120 min was 88% of the initial dye concentration, which was 50 mg L-1. In other words, starting from 50 mg L-1, most of the dye had been removed from the water after 120 min at that dose.

It should be noted that the Langmuir fit is reported for pH 6 only. Capacity at other pH values was not measured, so it cannot be assumed that the same capacity of 163 mg of dye per gram of adsorbent would apply at a different pH. The results therefore rest on batch tests at 298 K, an initial dye concentration of 50 mg L-1, and, for the removal figure, an adsorbent dose of 0.7 g L-1 and a contact time of 120 min. Taken together, the fitted capacity at pH 6 and the removal of 88% after 120 min describe the performance of the metal-organic framework under the stated conditions, and any comparison with another adsorbent should be made on the same basis of temperature, pH, dose, initial concentration and contact time as those used in these batch tests.
