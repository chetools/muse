A pilot amine absorption unit treated gas from a cement kiln for 60 days at 13 t of carbon dioxide per day. The unit captured 90% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas over the 60 days. Steam demand was 3.2 gigajoules per tonne of carbon dioxide captured. Solvent loss was 2 kg per tonne captured. Capture, steam demand and solvent loss are thus reported for the same unit over the same 60-day period, on cement kiln gas, at the stated rate.

The capture figure is an average over the treated gas of the whole period, at 13 t of carbon dioxide per day. Steam demand and solvent loss are stated per tonne captured, so the three figures share the captured tonne as the basis of the two demand figures and the period as the basis of the capture figure. No figure is reported for a period beyond 60 days, and no figure is reported at a second rate.

The tested scope ends at 60 days. Beyond 60 days, the effect of kiln dust on the solvent is untested, and solvent behaviour under longer exposure to kiln dust is not reported. Within the tested period, the unit captured 90% of the carbon dioxide in the treated gas at a steam demand of 3.2 gigajoules per tonne captured and a solvent loss of 2 kg per tonne captured.
