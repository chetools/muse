The Reynolds number Re is computed from density rho (kg m-3), mean velocity v (m s-1), pipe diameter D (m) and dynamic viscosity mu (Pa s) by Re = rho v D / mu. Substitution of these inputs into Re = rho v D / mu gives Re = 998 x 2.1 x 0.025 / 0.001. Thus Re = 52400. The value is dimensionless, as the units of rho, v, D and mu cancel in the ratio.
