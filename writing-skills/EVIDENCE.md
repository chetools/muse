# Evidence and limits

## Method

Each rule was coded from a measured corpus: documents were read against a fixed rubric, constructions were counted, and a rule states a construction that the coded documents use at the stated scale. Advice literature was not a source for the main rules. Two codebook anchors for the business corpus (Barbara Minto, *The Pyramid Principle*; Gene Zelazny, *Say It with Charts*) name forms the corpus documents use; the rules state the measured forms, not the books' advice.

## Corpora

STEM papers: 37 papers from *Nature* and *Science*, top citation rank, 2015–2024; an 8-paper mid-cited comparison set from the same journals. Flow measures (sentence openings, connectives, sentence lengths) were computed on 34 of the 37 papers.

Instructional writing: 22 items from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and DeepMind (tutorials, explainers, and documentation).

Distinctive patterns: 26 further *Nature* and *Science* papers and 16 further instructional and research-blog items (Anthropic, DeepMind, OpenAI, Google Research, Microsoft Research, Meta AI, Distill, and independent research blogs), coded for low-frequency constructions that carry a result, a scope condition, and an interpretation with no loss in one move. Rules 15–22 rest in part on this pass.

Equations: an oversample of 10 equation-bearing papers, 19 coded equations, 103 symbol groups. Rules E1–E4 rest on this pass and on the main corpus.

Business and consulting: 22 documents. Six long-form articles from *Harvard Business Review* and *MIT Sloan Management Review*; seven documents from McKinsey & Company, the McKinsey Global Institute, Boston Consulting Group, and Bain & Company; five chief executive officer letters (Berkshire Hathaway, JPMorgan Chase, Amazon); a 4-document comparison set from the mid-readership business press.

## Paired tests

Specimen pairs were written at equal word counts, one version with a skill and one version without a skill, on identical briefs, and coded blind with labels randomised. Anti-pattern lists (What not to do) record forms that occurred in the no-skill versions at the stated counts while the with-skill versions were favoured on the same feature.

A later test compared the combined file in `SKILL.md` against the two predecessor skills used separately, on five decision cases (a STEM tutorial, a STEM abstract, a recommendation memo, an executive summary, a slide storyline) and two diagnostic cases (an equation passage, a chief executive officer letter). The combined version was judged more effective in three of the five decision cases; its losses produced the amendments marked "[Test amendment]" in `SKILL.md`. The diagnostic loss on the letter case is the measured cost of holding the letter patterns in the appendix: one case, decided on features that the main business rules partly cover.

Case codes in the "[Test amendment]" marks in `SKILL.md`: T1 is the tutorial case, T2 the abstract case, T3 the recommendation memo case, T4 the executive summary case, T5 the slide storyline case; D1 is the equation diagnostic case and D2 the letter diagnostic case. One mark cites a case from an earlier paired test of a shortened draft of the STEM skill (the slim test): S6 in that test is an equation passage, and its verdict agrees with D1 on symbol placement.

A long-form pair stands in `EXAMPLES.md` (Pair 3): a route-comparison report at 1,200 words per version, written from one frozen fact base, one version under the combined file and one version with no skill. Coded contrasts for the pair are stated in `EXAMPLES.md` as checks against rule numbers. The pair is a demonstration specimen, not a blind test: the versions were not coded blind, and no verdict beyond the stated feature counts is recorded for it.

## Limits

- The pilots measure text: coded features and coder verdicts on those features. No pilot measures reader outcomes, comprehension, or decision quality.
- Each condition in the paired tests ran once per case. A three-to-two split across five cases does not exclude ordinary variation.
- The combined-skill test used one coder and reports no second-coder reliability statistic. The business pilot reports no second-coder reliability statistic.
- Frequencies are frequencies over the coded sets named here. A construction frequent in these corpora is not thereby frequent in a field corpus, and a rule states a form to use, not a rate to match.
- Corpus documents are published works under their publishers' copyright and are not included in this package. Rules, counts at corpus level, and the constructed specimens in `EXAMPLES.md` are the package's content.
- These are working rules. A rule is revised when a further coded corpus or a paired test counts against it.
