## Independent review (clean context; specification, texts and
figure only) and adjudication

The reviewer reproduced every headline state from the table and
full simulations (saturation data; pressures and the 5.6 ratio;
isothermal work 145 / 444 kW; first-pass outlets 195 / 214 /
292 K; first liquid 3.13 h at cold end 109.2 K; end state
105.96 K, 0.0170 kg/s, 377.9 kg, approaches 12.24 / 21.52 K;
valve-only stall 282.1 K; 20 MPa run 0.55 h, 171.4 K,
0.0834 kg/s; yield ceilings; turbine-fraction thresholds;
schematic connections) and raised six findings. Dispositions:

1. DEFECT, accepted — Theory §T2 Joule–Thomson coefficients were
   ten times too large (23 / 48 / 81 / 175 K/MPa printed; the
   conversion from 0.23 / 0.48 / 0.81 / 1.75 K/bar had multiplied
   by 100 instead of 10, and §T2 contradicted its own 1 K valve
   drop). Corrected to 2.3 / 4.8 / 8.1 / 17.5 K/MPa; the reviewer
   independently reproduced the valve drops (1.01 K from 293 K).
2. DEFECT, accepted — the model's turbine exhaust is two-phase
   at cold inlets (vapour fraction 0.946 at the design end state,
   about 5% liquid by mass; verified by direct recomputation:
   exhaust enthalpy 203.3 kJ/kg at cold end 106 K), which the
   Process panel's unqualified "a turbine cannot run on a
   two-phase mixture" contradicted. Process now states the
   liquid-rich-stream limit with the exhaust qualification, and
   §T3 states the exhaust state and its size with numbers.
3. QUESTIONABLE, accepted — the "expander outlet temperature"
   (87.9 K) is the flow-weighted mean of the turbine (84.29 K)
   and valve (98.75 K) outlets, not the temperature of the
   combined stream (two-phase at 84.4 K in the separator). The
   Fig. P2 caption now defines it as the flow-weighted mean at
   its first use in reading order.
4. QUESTIONABLE, accepted — Safety asserted oxygen enrichment
   "in the separator of this loop", an effect the pseudo-pure
   model does not compute. A scope sentence now states the
   enrichment is a property of real air and the model's liquid
   has the feed composition.
5. QUESTIONABLE (minor), accepted — the low-pressure vapour
   specific heat capacity in the 20 MPa explanation was the
   568 kPa value (1.04); at the return pressure (138 kPa, 171 K)
   CoolProp gives 1.012. Corrected to 1.01 kJ/(kg K); the dense
   feed value 1.97 was verified.
6. QUESTIONABLE (minor), accepted — §T1 printed enthalpies
   (425.6 / 426.7) differ by 1.1 against the stated 1.0 kJ/kg
   difference. Enthalpies now printed to two decimals (425.64 /
   426.66) and the difference as 1.02 kJ/kg.
