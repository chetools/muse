8   Checks

Initial conditions. By construction through Table 2 and Table 3, every entry of Table 4 satisfies y(0)=0y(0) = 0y(0)=0 and y′(0)=0y'\left(0\right) = 0y′(0)=0, where y′(0)y'\left(0\right)y′(0) denotes the first derivative of y(t)y(t)y(t) with respect to ttt, evaluated at t=0t = 0t=0. The step entries of Table 1 likewise satisfy y(0)=0y(0) = 0y(0)=0 and y′(0)=0y'\left(0\right) = 0y′(0)=0.

Long-time behaviour. For ζ>0\zeta > 0ζ>0 every transient term decays as ttt increases without bound, so each sinusoidal response approaches its steady-state part of Equation (4) or Table 5, and each step response of Table 1 approaches KMKMKM.

Frequency limits. At qqq approaching 0, Equation (2) gives RRR approaching 1 and ϕ\phiϕ approaching 0, and the sinusoidal steady-state parts approach the response to a constant input of magnitude MMM, consistent with Table 1. At qqq increasing without bound, RRR decreases as 1/q21/q^{2}1/q2, so the steady-state amplitude decreases as KM/q2KM/q^{2}KM/q2.
