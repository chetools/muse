## What changed

- Source of record established: no source notebook survived in the
  repository. The page existed only as the two published exports in
  `muse-repo-pages/fopdt-fitting/`, which embedded two slightly
  different versions of the notebook. The source was extracted from
  the published `index.html` — the variant carrying the later partial
  revision — its prose was adopted as the baseline, and the project

  export route.
- Full prose rewrite of `fopdt_fitting.py` under the writing skill:
  the opening now states the tutorial's goal, its audience and the
  default run's outcome with values (K = 1.492, τ = 7.633 min,
  θ = 3.115 min against the true 1.5, 8.0 min, 3.0 min); the data are
  stated to be simulated, generated by the model at the true values
  with Gaussian noise of standard deviation σ at a fixed seed; the
  single display is numbered (1) and referred to by number; the
  fitting-strategy paragraph names the routines (scipy's `curve_fit`
  and `minimize_scalar`), the grid (21 θ values to half the record
  length), the starting values, the bounds and the refinement
  bracket; a closing Interpretation and check section states what
  the default fit shows and the two noise checks; a References
  section was added (Seborg et al. 2016, §7.2; Virtanen et al.
  2020), both entries verified against publisher records during the
  rewrite.
- Mechanism correction N1, applied as the delta fix and verified at
  delta re-review: the strategy paragraph's claim that the sum of
  squared errors is piecewise constant in θ between sample times was
  false of the code (θ enters the model continuously). The paragraph
  now states the verified mechanism: the SSE is continuous in θ but
  not smooth, its slope changing abruptly at every sample time.
- Browser title (audit F19), fixed at build: both exports carry the
  page's heading as their `<title>`, set by post-export replacement
  as on tube — a fresh marimo 0.25.1 export derives "fopdt fitting"
  from the filename, and the previously published files carried
  "fopdt wasm demo".
