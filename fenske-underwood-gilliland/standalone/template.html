Fenske–Underwood–Gilliland (FUG) Shortcut Distillation

Shortcut design of a multicomponent distillation column by the Fenske equation (minimum stages), the Underwood equations (minimum reflux) and the Gilliland correlation (stages at the operating reflux), with a feed-stage location. The worked case is the debutanizer of Seader, Henley & Roper, Separation Process Principles, 4th ed. (2016), Chapter 9. All calculations are performed in Python (Pyodide). If the Python runtime cannot load, an explicit error is shown, the precomputed default case remains visible, and no calculation is performed by JavaScript.

Feed, volatilities and splits

Component order is decreasing volatility. LK denotes the light key (n-butane) and HK the heavy key (isopentane). Feed flow rates f are in lbmol/h (pound-moles per hour). Mean relative volatility, alpha, is dimensionless, relative to the heavy key, whose value is 1.
