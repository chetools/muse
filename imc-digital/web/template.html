Digital internal model control

Internal model control (IMC) implemented on a digital controller: the conversion of the process transfer function to discrete form by the zero-order hold (ZOH), the trapezoidal conversion of the controller, and the sampled closed loop with output limits and anti-windup. All signals are dimensionless deviations from a steady operating point; times are in seconds (s). Readers are assumed to know Laplace transforms and transfer functions; no digital-control background is assumed, and sampling, the hold and the difference equations are developed from the continuous forms. At the default model (process gain 1, time constant 10 s, dead time 2 s, filter time constant 2 s, sampling period 0.4 s), the first commanded manipulated variable is 4.6364, clipped to the delivered value 3 by the default output limit; the output first moves at sample 6, and the nominal cycle with the limits set wide reaches 0.6333 at t = 4.0 s. In the walkthrough's limited case (maximum output 1.5), anti-windup holds the overshoot at 0 % against 16.18 % without it.
