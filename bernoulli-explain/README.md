# Bernoulli Explain

A standalone page for a recorded-explanation assessment: the student explains the Bernoulli equation aloud (or in writing) over 11 numbered slides. The system energy balance from a textbook figure is built up incrementally (system side, inlet and outlet, heat and work done on the system as + dW with subscript ext), Pv is replaced by P/rho, enthalpy is substituted, frictional heating enters on the right side with a negative sign, and setting frictional heating and external work to zero leaves an unlabelled final equation for the student to name. No symbol definitions appear on the slides; defining them aloud is the student's task. Record mode makes one continuous take with every move timestamped; live transcription (Web Speech API) with a per-step term list and a correction map runs during the take. After the take, the page builds a final video — slide, vertical face strip from the camera recording, and the corrected transcript burned in as captions — which the student downloads and uploads to YouTube as an unlisted video.

`bernoulli-explain.html` is the whole app (no build step). The published page is the same bytes at `bernoulli-explain/index.html` on the `gh-pages` branch: https://chetools.github.io/muse/bernoulli-explain/
