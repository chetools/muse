Adequate for the page's derivation, labelled an estimate.
- Compressor stage check: overall pressure ratio 6.2 in three stages gives a stage ratio of 1.837; isentropic stage outlet from 293 K = 348.7 K; at a stage efficiency of 0.75 the outlet is 367 K (94 degrees C). A final-stage outlet of 175 degrees C is not consistent with a three-stage intercooled machine.

The actual-slate value agrees with the 51-53 kWh/t range already cited on the page [8].
