# Research Memo C — Safety (subagent, 2026-10-24; full text in chat handoff)

Controlling safety case: hydrocarbon management in liquid oxygen (LOX).
- Acetylene precipitates as shock-sensitive solid at 4-6 ppm in LOX [AIGA 056/14 via Scribd re-hosting, unofficial copy].
- EIGA Doc 65 maximum allowable concentrations in downflow-reboiler LOX at 1.2 bar abs (ppm by volume): acetylene 0.5; total hydrocarbons (as methane) 500; methane 500; ethylene 15; ethane 15; propylene 1; propane 1; C4 hydrocarbons 1; carbon dioxide 0.5; nitrous oxide 5. [Gas Association of India hosting of EIGA Doc 65 / AIGA 035 family table]
- Continuous LOX purge 0.1-0.2% of incoming air for gas-only plants, or equivalent at least every 8 h [EIGA Doc 65/20, eiga.eu].
- Concentration factor ~500 for purge-only LOX production vs ~5 for internal-compression cycle [EIGA Doc 65/20].
- Dry boiling in thermosyphon reboiler passages: second hydrocarbon-rich phase / deposits when recirculation lost [AIGA 035/06].
- Incidents: Fushun, China, 16 May 1997 — 4 killed, 4 severely injured, 27 slightly; ethylene pollution peak + low vaporiser level; aluminium in LOX as fuel [ARIA No. 11603]. Bintulu, Malaysia, 25 Dec 1997 — 12 injured, blast damage to 5 km; forest-fire hydrocarbon aerosol + nitrous oxide crystal fouling; aluminium combustion [van Hardeveld et al., J. Loss Prev. Process Ind. 2001; ARIA No. 23132]. Yima, China, 19 July 2019 — 15 killed, 15 seriously injured [The Chemical Engineer, IChemE]; cause (continued operation with known cold-box leak, crack 12 July) PARTIALLY VERIFIED (secondary republication).
- Oxygen deficiency: entry window 19.5% min / 23.5% max oxygen by volume [EIGA Doc 154/16]; effects table [EIGA Doc 44/18]. Clothing ventilate 15 min after enrichment exposure [EIGA Doc 23.03].
- Oxygen fires: EIGA Doc 04 (fire hazards of oxygen-enriched atmospheres); Doc 127 bulk storage (oil/grease exclusion, cleaning per Doc 33); tin bronze vs aluminium bronze trap; PTFE tape caution [Doc 04].
- Expansion ratios on vaporisation: nitrogen 696, argon 847, oxygen 862 [Wayne State University cryogenic liquid safety] — thermal relief on every liquid-full isolatable section.
- Materials: stainless/austenitic steels, 9% nickel steel, copper alloys, aluminium; PTFE seals [EIGA Doc 127 §4.6].
- Sieve dusting: adsorbent particles 1-5 mm, prone to dusting if incorrectly operated; after-filter required [EIGA Doc 147/22, eiga.eu]. Liquid-water carryover can drive bed temperatures >100 C [Doc 147].
- Perlite intrusion into process equipment = explosion precursor; keep process pressure above purge pressure after leak identified [AIGA 079/18].
- Codes verified: EIGA Doc 147 (= AIGA 056 / CGA P-8 family), Doc 65 (= CGA P-8.4), Doc 13 / CGA G-4.4 (oxygen piping), Doc 33 / CGA G-4.1 (cleaning), Doc 04, Doc 44, Doc 154, Doc 127, Doc 94 (environmental); CGA G-4.8 (aluminium structured packing), G-4.9 (brazed aluminium exchangers) — both post-Fushun/Bintulu; CGA P-12; CGA S-1 PRD series; ASME BPVC Section VIII; ASME B31.3; NFPA 53; ASTM G04 family (G63, G72, G74, G88, G93, G94, G126, G128); API RP 520/521 (general methodology); OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146.
- Energy: ~250 kWh/t (0.36 kWh/Nm3) journal-stated typical for modern double-column [Energy Convers. Manag. 2021]; 0.40-0.59 kWh/Nm3 traditional range [Energy 2024] — quote with basis. No universal CO2/t factor.
