# Research Memo B — Production scale and economics (subagent, 2026-10-24)

Key verified points (full memo in chat handoff 2026-10-24):
- Linde customized plants: 1,000 to 5,500 tonnes/day oxygen (30,000 to 165,000 Nm3/h). Jamnagar: five ASUs at 5,250 t/d each. Ras Laffan complex: eight ASUs, 30,000 t/d total. Source: Linde Engineering customized ASU page.
- Linde packaged plants: up to 850 t/d oxygen (25,000 Nm3/h) or 2,000 t/d nitrogen per one edition; another edition states 13,000 Nm3/h oxygen / 30,000 Nm3/h nitrogen — current ceiling UNVERIFIED between editions.
- Specific power: typical cryogenic >200 kWh/t O2; thermodynamic minimum ~51-53 kWh/t (Int. J. Environ. Res. Public Health 2021, 18, 10370). NETL low-pressure ASU at 99.5% purity: 204.5 kWh/t. Oxyfuel study: 200 kWh/t at 95% purity, 240 kWh/t at 99.5%. Messer CryoGOX (90-98%, internal compression): 0.40-0.55 kWh/Nm3.
- Electricity share: 60-70% of total production costs (Emergen market report); 65-75% of lifecycle OPEX (vendor guide); up to 70-80% of operating cost (IndexBox). Conclusion robust, exact value source-dependent.
- CAPEX points: NLMK Lipetsk ~EUR 100M for ~72,000 Nm3/h GOX (2018, Chemical Engineering); Yantai Wanhua ~EUR 130M for two 55,000 Nm3/h plants (2011). Emergen range USD 100-500M large facilities (low authority). NO sourced CAPEX-capacity correlation or scaling exponent found — UNVERIFIED. Do not present 0.6-0.7 exponent as ASU-sourced; if used in the model, label as a general engineering assumption of the model.
- DOE SBIR: below 500 t/d cryogenic CAPEX/energy uncompetitive vs PSA/VPSA; PSA at 10-50 t/d ~USD 200/t, 300-500 kWh/t.
- Business models (Air Products 2022 annual report): on-site sale-of-gas contracts typically 10-15 years with fixed monthly charges/minimum purchase/escalators; merchant contracts generally five years or less, no minimums.
- Argon prices (Procurement Resource 2026): China ~USD 675/t, India ~USD 725-763/t (Q2 2026), volatile.
