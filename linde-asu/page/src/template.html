Process flow diagram PFD-102

Drawing PFD-102, Revision 0: Linde nitrogen–oxygen air separation unit with crude argon recovery, drawn under specification PFD-SPEC-001 with symbols from the traced symbol library. Stream values on the drawing are sample values on a basis of 1000 kmol/h of feed air, where kmol/h denotes kilomoles per hour; the balances of the displayed values close on every unit.
