Report (transformed)

Packed-column absorption of carbon dioxide into aqueous monoethanolamine: Pall rings and gauze structured packing at matched conditions

Abstract

Post-combustion capture removes carbon dioxide from exhaust gas by absorption into a solvent, and packed columns provide gas–solvent contact in many capture units. A bench comparison of two packings in one column, under identical feed and solvent conditions, was undertaken because published ratings for structured packing rest on measurements made under differing conditions. One glass column was charged with Pall rings and then with gauze structured packing, and capture, mass transfer and wet pressure drop were measured for each charge over the same ranges of gas and liquid rate. At a reference condition, the structured packing captured more carbon dioxide and transferred more carbon dioxide per unit volume at a far lower wet pressure drop than the Pall rings (capture fraction 0.91 against 0.78; overall volumetric mass-transfer coefficient 0.85 against 0.58 mol m−3 s−1 kPa−1; wet pressure drop 350 against 920 Pa m−1). Rich solvent left the column at a higher loading with the structured packing (0.27 against 0.25 mol/mol). Pressure drop separated the packings by a wider factor than capture did, near 2.6 against near 1.2. Bench screening before pilot work can rank the two packings at the conditions tested.

1 Introduction

Gas-fired and cement exhaust are sources for post-combustion capture, in which carbon dioxide is absorbed from a gas stream into a liquid solvent after combustion. Aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA, an amine solvent that reacts with carbon dioxide in solution) is the solvent used here. Packing provides the surface over which gas and liquid meet, and packing choice affects three quantities: the fraction of incoming carbon dioxide captured, the mass-transfer rate that sets packed height, and the pressure drop that sets blower power.

Ratings for structured packing are reported at conditions that differ from study to study. A ranking of a random packing and a structured packing in the same column, at one gas composition and one solvent concentration, appears to be missing. Whether the structured packing advantage persists under matched conditions, and on which quantity it is largest, is unknown.

Measurements reported here address that gap. One column was operated with each packing in turn over a grid of gas and liquid rates, and an extended run examined how capture changed with time on the structured packing.

2 Methods

Column and packings

Column internal diameter D (m) was 0.075 m, and packed height Z (m) was 1.20 m. Gas and liquid passed countercurrently through the glass column. Random packing was stainless-steel Pall rings of nominal size 0.016 m. Structured packing was stainless-steel gauze with a nominal specific surface area of 500 m2 m−3.

Feed, solvent and reference condition

Fresh solvent was aqueous MEA at a mass fraction of 0.30, with a lean loading (mol of carbon dioxide per mol of MEA in solvent entering the column) of 0.18 mol/mol. Feed gas was carbon dioxide in nitrogen, with inlet mole fraction yin (dimensionless) of 0.12 and no oxygen, sulfur dioxide or particulate matter. Reference operation used a gas superficial velocity uG (m s−1, gas volumetric flow divided by the empty column cross-sectional area) of 0.85 m s−1 and a liquid superficial velocity uL (m s−1, defined in the same way for liquid) of 0.0085 m s−1. Inlet temperatures were 40 °C for gas and for lean solvent, and pressure was 101 kPa.

Run programme and measurement

Each packing was run at 24 conditions, with uG from 0.55 to 1.15 m s−1 and uL from 0.004 to 0.013 m s−1, each measured three times (n = 3). Steady state was judged by an outlet mole fraction that changed by less than 0.002 over 600 s.

Inlet and outlet carbon dioxide mole fractions were measured with an infrared analyser (range 0 to 0.20, stated accuracy 0.003), liquid loading by acid titration, temperature at five positions by thermocouples, and pressure drop by a differential pressure transducer. Analyser zero was checked each day. One run was discarded and repeated after a leak was found in a sampling line.

Quantities computed from the measurements

Capture fraction (dimensionless) is the inlet molar flow of carbon dioxide minus the outlet molar flow, divided by the inlet molar flow. Rich loading (mol of carbon dioxide per mol of MEA in solvent leaving the column) follows from titration of that liquid.

Equation (1) gives KGa (mol m−3 s−1 kPa−1) from the molar absorption rate N (mol s−1), the column cross-sectional area A (m2), calculated from D, the packed height Z (m) and the log-mean carbon dioxide partial-pressure difference Δplm (kPa):

KGa = N / (AZΔplm)(1)

Here KG (mol m−2 s−1 kPa−1) is the overall gas-phase coefficient and a (m2 m−3) is the effective interfacial area per unit packed volume. Only the product KGa was obtained; KG and a were not measured separately. Wet pressure drop per unit packed height (Pa m−1) is the measured drop divided by Z, with liquid flowing.

3 Results

Reference condition

At the reference condition, capture fraction was 0.91 (standard deviation, SD, 0.02; n = 3) for the structured packing and 0.78 (SD 0.03; n = 3) for the Pall rings. Rich loading was 0.27 mol/mol for the structured packing against 0.25 mol/mol for the Pall rings.

Values of KGa at the reference condition were 0.85 mol m−3 s−1 kPa−1 (SD 0.05) for the structured packing and 0.58 mol m−3 s−1 kPa−1 (SD 0.04) for the Pall rings. Column temperature peaked part-way down the packing for both charges, at 58 °C at about 0.35 of the packed height from the top for the structured packing and at 52 °C for the Pall rings.

Wet pressure drop per unit packed height at the reference condition was 350 Pa m−1 (SD 30 Pa m−1) for the structured packing and 920 Pa m−1 (SD 80 Pa m−1) for the Pall rings.

Variation with gas and liquid rate

Capture increased with liquid rate for both packings. At the highest rate tested, uL = 0.013 m s−1, capture fraction was 0.93 for the structured packing and 0.88 for the Pall rings, a narrower gap than at the reference condition.

Capture fraction decreased as gas rate increased. At uG = 1.15 m s−1, capture fraction was 0.82 for the structured packing against 0.64 for the Pall rings.

Pressure drop for the Pall rings increased sharply above uL = 0.011 m s−1 at uG = 1.15 m s−1, the loading behaviour that precedes flooding. No sharp increase was detected for the structured packing up to uG = 1.15 m s−1 and uL = 0.013 m s−1; flooding beyond that range was not measured.

Repeatability for the structured packing was worse at uL = 0.004 m s−1, where capture fraction had an SD of 0.05 against 0.02 at the reference condition. Liquid distribution was not measured.

Extended run on the structured packing

During 120 h at the reference condition, capture fraction for the structured packing decreased from 0.91 to 0.87. Solvent colour darkened, and solvent viscosity, measured at 40 °C, increased by 8 %. MEA loss was not quantified, and no degradation product was analysed.

4 Discussion

Pressure drop separates the two packings most clearly. Capture for the Pall rings approached capture for the structured packing at the highest liquid rate (0.88 against 0.93), and higher liquid rate is a route to higher capture for either packing. Wet pressure drop stayed higher for the Pall rings by a factor near 2.6. Packing choice is a trade among capture, column size through KGa, and blower power through pressure drop.

Cost runs in the opposite direction. Structured packing costs more per unit packed volume than the Pall rings, and neither packing was priced. No ranking on total cost follows.

Higher rich loading for the structured packing (0.27 against 0.25 mol/mol) corresponds to a lower regeneration energy in a derived estimate: 3.6 GJ per tonne of carbon dioxide against 4.1 GJ per tonne for the Pall rings, estimated from rich loading with a steam correlation. Regeneration was not operated, the correlation source was not recorded, and both values are estimates.

Observations at the lowest liquid rate suggest a distribution problem for the structured packing. Incomplete wetting, wall flow, or the distributor shared by both packings could each produce the larger scatter (SD 0.05 against 0.02 at the reference condition), and distribution was not measured.

Solvent change during the extended run is consistent with degradation or accumulation. Capture decreased (0.91 to 0.87 over 120 h), viscosity increased by 8 %, and colour darkened, while MEA loss and degradation products went unmeasured. A specific cause is unknown. Degradation with the tested gas, which had no oxygen, may understate degradation in service.

Wall effects limit transfer of the absolute values to larger columns. A column of diameter 0.075 m has a larger wall influence than an industrial column, and the values here rank the two packings at bench scale.

5 Conclusions

At one gas composition (inlet carbon dioxide mole fraction 0.12), one solvent concentration (MEA mass fraction 0.30) and one column diameter (0.075 m), the structured packing gave a higher capture fraction than the Pall rings (0.91 against 0.78), a higher KGa (0.85 against 0.58 mol m−3 s−1 kPa−1), a lower wet pressure drop (350 against 920 Pa m−1) and a higher rich loading (0.27 against 0.25 mol/mol). The comparison is limited to bench scale, to a gas with no oxygen, sulfur dioxide or particulate matter, and to the rates tested. Regeneration energy was estimated, not measured, packing cost was not measured, and no ranking on total cost is made. Degradation over 120 h appeared as a decrease in capture with darkening and a viscosity increase, with no cause assigned.

Work remaining

  • Temperature resolution: five thermocouples locate the temperature maximum only approximately. Added positions along the packed height would fix the peak value and position.
  • Liquid distribution: capture repeatability for the structured packing at uL = 0.004 m s−1 is poor and its cause is untested. A distribution measurement, or a trial with a second distributor, would test the wetting, wall-flow and distributor explanations.
  • Flooding limit: the structured packing was not operated to flooding. Extending rates beyond the range tested would bound the range over which the comparison stands.
  • Energy estimate: the steam correlation behind the derived regeneration energies was not recorded. Identifying it would make the estimate checkable, and operating regeneration would replace the estimate with a measurement.
  • Degradation chemistry: the 120 h run records solvent change with no quantified MEA loss and no product analysis. A run on the scale of months, with MEA loss quantified and products analysed, would assign a cause.
  • Error analysis: propagation of the analyser accuracy (0.003 mole fraction) into KGa was not completed. Completing it would state the total uncertainty of KGa in place of the repeat standard deviation alone.
  • Pall rings reference: the Pall rings reference condition was not re-measured when the structured reference condition was repeated. Re-measurement would exclude rig drift from the comparison.
  • Second concentration and costing: the comparison rests on one MEA mass fraction (0.30) and on no costing. A second concentration would test whether the ranking changes with solvent strength, and pricing both packings per unit packed volume would give a cost comparison.

How a transformation with this skill is done

Summary

The source text is frozen first, and nothing is added to it. The text is then split into units, one claim with its support in each, and the units are ranked and ordered by dependency, so that definitions and method stand before the results that rest on them. Duplicates are merged with every qualifier kept, and material of low rank is kept at the end of its passage rather than cut. The opening and the closing are sized to the body. Where the target is a new STEM report, the draft is made in a fixed drafting order: the fact base is listed first, with each item labelled given, measured, fitted, estimated or assumed; the advance is stated in one sentence; the results are drafted in the order of their claim sentences; the closing is drafted before the opening; the abstract is drafted last among the text parts, and the title last. The ordered material is then written under the core rules of the package; a full rewrite, where a change of document form is requested, is made under the whole package: a fixed order for the abstract and the opening of a STEM document, symbols and terms defined at first use, values stated with their conditions and comparators, limits stated inside the claims they bound, one name for each object, and a closing held at the strength of the evidence. A revision pass checks the draft against the source claim by claim, and a document of consequence is then checked by a reader who did not write it. What the source does not settle is reported, not resolved by invention. The case record for the two columns above — units, ranks, moves and merges — stands in the next section; the wording changes stand in the section after it; the points this transformation could not settle stand in the section after that.

Details

  1. Load by document type. The core rules load for all writing under the package. Each reference file loads only when its condition holds. For this transformation the loaded files were the core; the drafting reference, because a new STEM report was drafted from a fact base; the structure reference, because a reorganization was requested and the body exceeds about 300 words; the textbook and equations references, because the target document carries detail sentences and a displayed equation; the lexicon and exemplars references, loaded with the drafting reference and again at the check pass; and the revision and check reference, with its examples file, for the check passes. The figures reference did not load: the target document has no figure or table. The business reference did not load: the target is a STEM report, not a business document. The significance reference did not load: the report is written for readers inside its own subfield, not for a general-science journal or for readers outside the subfield.
  2. Freeze the source. The notes were fixed as the only source of facts before any drafting. No fact, consequence or causal link may be stated that the source does not state, and a derived figure is stated as derived, with its derivation. Every later check runs against the frozen source.
  3. Inventory the units. The source is split into units, each one claim with the sentences that support it, and each unit is tagged by role — thesis, claim, evidence, definition, example, limit or aside — with its place in the source recorded. The inventory is a working record; it does not enter the document.
  4. State the thesis from the source. The principal claim is stated in one sentence taken from the source. Where the source states no principal claim, or states two that compete, that finding is reported and no thesis is invented. The record for this case, including the two competing conclusions in the notes, stands under “How the organisation was done”.
  5. Rank the units. Each unit is ranked principal, supporting or peripheral on three grounds, in order: whether the thesis requires it, how many other units depend on it, and the weight of its evidence. A rank that rests on judgement rather than on a dependency is marked as a judgement in the record.
  6. List the dependencies and order the body. A unit requires another when it uses a term, symbol, premise, method or result that the other unit introduces; every such requirement is listed as a pair. The body order is a topological order of those pairs, with rank as the tie-break: no unit stands before a unit it requires, and among units free to move the higher rank stands first. Units on one topic stand together in one passage, unless a dependency splits them. Implementation detail, where a document has any, stands last.
  7. Merge duplicates; place peripheral units. Two units stating the same claim are merged into one, and the merged unit keeps every qualifier, number and condition of both. A peripheral unit is kept, moved to the end of its passage or into the closing limits, and listed in the record; it is cut only on request.
  8. Size the opening and the closing. Opening and closing scale with the word count of the body: one sentence each below about 300 words; one paragraph each from about 300 to about 1,500 words; up to three paragraphs, or a headed section, above that size. Each stays near 5–15% of the body. Where a document type fixes the content of the opening and the closing — the abstract order and the opening order for STEM text, in this case — the type frame governs the content and the size bands govern the length. The closing restates the principal claim at its tested scope, with its limits named, and adds no new claim.
  9. Write under the core rules. Where only an order was requested, body sentences keep their source wording and edits are limited to the opening, the closing, transitions and broken joins. A change of document form, as here from notebook fragments to a report, is a full rewrite, made only on request and then under the whole package. The rules that carry most of the change in this case are: formal, impersonal register and the field's own terms; the STEM opening order (setting, established knowledge, gap, aim, method preview) and abstract order (setting, aim, method, principal result, interpretation, implication, with no limitation in the abstract); every symbol defined at or before first use, with its unit where a unit applies, and every abbreviation expanded at first use (standard notation — element symbols, formulae of common species and SI unit symbols — takes no definition); one name for each object, restated rather than replaced by a synonym; each number bundled in its sentence with its qualifier, comparator and condition; the limit inside the claim sentence, before the number it qualifies, with validity stated for the law or model as subject; a null result stated as a detection statement over a stated range; no inference marker in a frame sentence; a term defined in place at its first use, by default in a parenthetical or appositive form; presenting verbs (show, find, observe) for what the record contains and inferring verbs (suggest, indicate) for an inference, at hedged strength; no announced closing; and interpretation placed next to the result it interprets.
  10. Run the revision pass. The writer revises the draft against the package in a fixed order: loading (the document type named, and exactly the references whose conditions hold loaded); fidelity to the source; notation; sentence openings and lengths; claims and limits; the document-type completion checks; the AI-writing patterns and the house word list, judged by density rather than by single occurrences; a mechanical scan by the package's lint tool, with each flag resolved or kept with a reason; a second pass on the revision, including a claim-by-claim fidelity diff; and a remove-10% pass that cuts any element whose removal loses no information.
  11. Run the independent check. A document of consequence is checked by a checker that did not write it, holding the frozen source, the draft and the package, and never the writer's reasoning. The checker works under eight heads — traceability, recomputation, literal truth, cross-part agreement, completeness against the brief, notation, citations, and regression after corrections — and returns findings without editing the draft. Each finding is adjudicated against the source before any correction is applied, and the corrected passages are checked again. The review record for this page, including the two balance failures the checkers found in the first draft of the dataset, stands in the covering message that delivered the page.
  12. Report what the source does not settle. A transformation under the package does not invent citations, data, figures or report front matter, and does not silently settle an ambiguity in the source. Each unsettled point is reported in the open, in this case in the “Uncertainties in the transformation” section, with the item a complete report would need.

How the organisation was done

Two units compete as the principal claim: a draft conclusion in the final entry states that the structured packing is a breakthrough that outperforms on every metric, and the entry itself then replaces that draft with a bounded version. The bounded version was taken as the thesis, because it is the claim the recorded values support, and the draft was not carried into the report. Each unit was ranked principal, supporting or peripheral by whether the thesis requires it, how many other units depend on it, and the weight of its evidence. Units were then ordered so that no unit stands before a unit it requires, with rank breaking ties: definitions and method stand before results, the coefficient is defined in Methods before the Results values that rest on it, and the derived energy estimate stands after the rich loading it is derived from. The report outline is: Abstract; 1 Introduction (setting, gap, aim); 2 Methods (column and packings; feed, solvent and reference condition; run programme and measurement; quantities computed from the measurements); 3 Results (reference condition; variation with gas and liquid rate; extended run); 4 Discussion; 5 Conclusions (claim at tested scope, limits); Work remaining (each item named by the gap it records).

How the writing was improved

  • One name for each object. The notes cycle between the full names and short forms — “rings”, “the rings”, a bare “structured” — and the structured packing enters as “gauze type”. The report uses “structured packing” and “Pall rings” throughout, so a value can be traced to its packing at every occurrence.
  • Values bundled with their conditions. The notes state “capture 91%!!” in one entry, its standard deviation in the same entry, its repeat count in the final entry, and its conditions in the first entry. The report states each principal value once per passage with its comparator in the same sentence, and with its standard deviation in the same sentence where the notes record one (the rich loading comparison carries no standard deviation in either text); the repeat count is stated once for all conditions in Methods and again with the capture values, and each condition is stated in the sentence or in the Methods statement that governs it.
  • One unit form for each quantity. Percentages, millimetres, minutes and “points” in the notes become fractions, metres, seconds and the same fraction form in the report (91% to 0.91; 75 mm to 0.075 m; 10 min to 600 s; pressure drop in Pa m−1 throughout).
  • Symbols defined at first use. The notes use KGa for a full entry before defining it, and define the area term afterwards in words (“the effective area bit”). In the report each symbol is defined with its unit at first use in the body, and the coefficient is defined by Eq. (1) in Methods, ahead of the Results values that rest on it; the Abstract states the principal values in summary form.
  • Claims held at the evidence. “Breakthrough”, “HUGE difference” and “outperforms on every metric” are replaced by the four measured comparisons, each with its values. A grading word stands only where its numbers stand in the same sentence.
  • Absence stated as a detection result. “Structured didn't do that” becomes “no sharp increase was detected for the structured packing up to uG = 1.15 m s−1 and uL = 0.013 m s−1”, with the range stated and flooding beyond that range stated as not measured; the notes' own caution, that the packing was never pushed to flooding, is carried by that form.
  • Inference separated from measurement. The wetting guess stays in the report as an inference at hedged strength — observations at the lowest liquid rate “suggest” a distribution problem — in the same passage as the statement that distribution was not measured. The distributor, named in the notes as an alternative cause, is named with it as a cause that could produce the same scatter.
  • Derived values labelled where they stand. The notes ask for the energy estimate to be marked “in big letters”. The report marks it in the sentence itself: the values stand in a “derived estimate”, the conclusions state that regeneration energy “was estimated, not measured”, and regeneration is stated as not operated.
  • Limits placed inside the closing claim. Limits scattered through the notes (one gas composition, no oxygen or dust, one diameter, cost unmeasured) are collected in Conclusions, and the closing claim opens with its scope. The abstract states the tested scope as method and carries no limitation statement, in the abstract order for a report; the limits stand in the closing.
  • Register and sentence form. Diary voice, dates, abbreviations of speech (“tbh”), exclamation marks and rhetorical questions are removed. Sentence openings vary across each passage, and no passage opens successive sentences with the same word.
  • Length. The report is longer than the notes (1,876 against 1,100 words). The added words are definitions at first use, conditions bundled with values, the abstract, and the closing restatement at scope. Shortening was applied to wording, not to qualifications, ranges or evidence.

Uncertainties in the transformation

Eight points could not be settled from the notes. Each point names what a real transformation would need before the report could be issued.

  1. Choice of thesis. The notes hold two competing conclusions in one entry. The bounded claim was taken because the same entry presents it as the correction of the draft, and because the recorded values support it and do not support the draft. An author could still intend a stronger claim on evidence held outside the notes; the choice needs author confirmation.
  2. Source of the regeneration-energy estimate. The notes cite “that steam correlation from the lit” and record that the reference was never written down. No citation can be written from the notes, so the report names no source and carries no reference list. A reference list built for this page would have had to invent entries. The estimate cannot be issued as a checked figure until the correlation is identified and checked, and the introduction's gap statement (point 3) has the same defect in weaker form.
  3. The gap claim is unverified. The introduction states that ratings for structured packing rest on measurements under differing conditions. That statement is the notes' impression (“everyone says structured is better”); no literature search was made for this transformation, so whether a same-charge comparison already exists in print is unknown.
  4. No figure can be drawn from the notes. The notes record reference values, range endpoints and standard deviations, but no run-by-run table for the 48 runs. A full report on this material would be expected to plot capture and pressure drop against gas and liquid rates. The dataset behind the notes would be needed; values were not reconstructed from the endpoints, because reconstructed points would be invented data.
  5. Uncertainty of the coefficient is incomplete. The standard deviations in the report are repeat standard deviations. Propagation of the analyser accuracy (0.003 mole fraction) into the coefficient was listed in the notes as unfinished and remains unfinished; the coefficient's total uncertainty is larger than the reported standard deviation by an amount the notes do not determine.
  6. Rig drift is not excluded for the rings. The structured-packing reference condition was re-measured in May and reproduced. The rings reference condition, measured in March, was not re-measured. The notes record that the March values stand and express no concern; the gap is flagged here rather than in the notes, because the structured reference condition was re-measured in May and the rings reference condition was not. The report reports the March values as standing; a reviewer could require the repeat.
  7. Report front matter is absent from the notes. Authorship, affiliations, funding and acknowledgements appear nowhere in the notes and none was invented. Length, abstract form and reference style also depend on who the report is written for, and no reader or commissioning body is named in the notes; a general report form was used, and the requirements of a named reader would take precedence over it.
  8. Two judgements in the ranking. The temperature peaks were ranked peripheral because no other unit depends on them, and the wetting explanation was ranked peripheral because it is unmeasured. Both ranks rest on judgement rather than on a dependency, and a different ranking would move the temperature passage into Discussion without changing any claim.

How this skill was created: key components

The skill is a package of writing rules for scientific, engineering, instructional and business text. Its components are a set of measured corpora, a coding method that turned the corpora into rules, a tiered package that loads the rules by document type, a check system, and an evidence file that records what each rule rests on and where its limits stand. The components are described here; the succession of versions is not.

Measured corpora

The rules were coded from documents, not taken from advice literature. Six corpora were measured. STEM papers: 37 papers from Nature and Science at top citation rank, 2015–2024, with an 8-paper mid-cited comparison set from the same journals, drawn in the pilot design as the check that the coded patterns are journal style rather than an artefact of citation rank; flow measures (sentence openings, connectives, sentence lengths) were computed on 34 of the 37 papers. Instructional writing: 22 items from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and DeepMind (tutorials, explainers and documentation). A distinctive-patterns pass: 26 further Nature and Science papers and 16 further instructional and research-blog items, coded for low-frequency constructions that state a result, a scope condition and an interpretation in one move; the rules on claims, limits and captions rest in part on this pass. Equations: an oversample of 10 equation-bearing papers, with 19 coded equations and 103 symbol groups. Business and consulting documents: 22 documents (long-form articles from Harvard Business Review and MIT Sloan Management Review; documents from McKinsey & Company, the McKinsey Global Institute, Boston Consulting Group and Bain & Company; five chief executive officer letters; and a 4-document comparison set from the mid-readership business press). Textbooks: a contrast study of five publicly available engineering textbooks, with detailed coding of three canonical chemical-engineering textbooks and stratified samples of 160 coded passages and 30 rewrite comparisons; a textbook rule stands only where the coded books agree. Later passes added three further measured sets: the editor-facing summaries of the two journals (24 items) and a title coding over 73 papers, for the rules on stating an advance; a cross-channel set (significance statements, review openings, lectures and further edited channels), coded for which constructions stand in every channel; and a lexical count over the combined corpora, which fixed the word-choice rules (431,116 words of corpus against a 10,327-word baseline for the one wholly new rule, on announced closings).

Coding method

Each corpus was read against a fixed rubric, constructions were counted, and a rule states a construction that the coded documents use at the stated scale. The pilot design's rubric covered abstract moves, opening moves, results text and per-figure coding including caption self-containment, per-equation coding including the fraction of symbols defined at or before first use, closing moves, paragraph connection, and computed surface measures with word lists for hedging and certainty fixed in advance. Two codebook anchors for the business corpus (Barbara Minto, The Pyramid Principle; Gene Zelazny, Say It with Charts) name forms the corpus documents use; the rules state the measured forms, not the books' advice. The pilot design set second-coder agreement thresholds for an item: retention at a kappa or intraclass correlation of at least 0.70, revision and retest at 0.60–0.69, and removal or splitting below 0.60.

Package architecture

The package is tiered so that a document loads only the rules its type needs. A core file of 37 rules loads for all writing under the package: register and words, source fidelity, notation, document structure, sentences and paragraphs, the STEM and instructional frames, claims and numbers, captions and citations, covering and commit messages, and a completion check. Reference files load by condition: textbook forms for detail sentences; equations, loaded with the textbook file, for a document with displayed equations; figures, for a document with figures, plots or tables; structure, for reorganizing existing text or outlining a document whose body exceeds about 300 words; business, for a business document; a drafting procedure, loaded when a new STEM paper or report is drafted from results or a fact base, which fixes the order in which the parts are drafted; a significance reference, loaded with the drafting procedure when a title, abstract or opening is drafted for readers outside the document's subfield or for a general-science journal; a lexicon of word choices where the corpora differentiate the choices, loaded with the drafting procedure and at a check pass; constructed exemplar passages, loaded with the drafting procedure and at a check pass, with a further set for model papers and combined-evidence closings; a Simplified Technical English reference, only when that style or a percentage of it is requested; a revision and check reference, with an examples file of rejected and accepted forms, at a check pass. An appendix holds two chief executive officer letter patterns outside the main rules, because they rest on one corpus. The evidence file is part of the package and is not loaded for writing.

House rules

Part of the package is not corpus-derived. A named set of rules comes from the author's standing style guide: the depth, document-structure and notation-roster rules in part, the number-and-unit typography rule, the citation rule, the figures reference, the Simplified Technical English reference, two of the independent-check heads, the field-term clause and the content-order clause, the rule against stating that a requirement has been met, the covering-message and commit-message rules, the house word list, the regression check head, and the tie-break clause of the sentence-openings rule. The size bands in the structure reference (about 300 and about 1,500 words, and 5–15% of the body) are house choices, not corpus measurements. The evidence file names which component each rule belongs to, so a reader can tell a measured rule from a house rule at every point.

Check system

Three instruments check work done under the package. A revision pass, run by the writer in a fixed order, ends with a fidelity diff of the revision against the source and a remove-10% pass. An independent check pass is run by a checker that did not write the draft, under eight heads from traceability to regression after corrections; the checker returns findings and does not edit. Three tools support the passes: a mechanical scan that flags candidate defects in a draft file for the writer to resolve or keep with a reason, a deterministic draft checker that applies the package's mechanical checks to a draft, and a package checker that verifies the package's own cross-references. Worked specimens — pairs of passages written with and without the rules from one shared fact base, with the differing forms highlighted — are published with the package as demonstrations of the rules in use.

Testing, and the limits recorded with the rules

The package was tested by paired comparison: specimen pairs written at equal word counts, one version with a skill and one without, on identical briefs, coded blind with labels randomised. A confirmation retest compared the tiered package against a single-file form of the same rules, and the tiered package was installed after that test. The evidence file records the limits of this base alongside the rules: the pilots measure text — coded features and coder verdicts — and no pilot measures reader outcomes, comprehension or decision quality; each paired-test condition ran once per case; the combined-skill test used one coder and reports no second-coder reliability statistic, and the business pilot reports no second-coder reliability statistic; and a construction frequent in the coded corpora is not thereby frequent in a field corpus. A copy of the package without its evidence file overstates what the rules rest on. The standing rule for revision is stated in the evidence file: a rule is revised when a further coded corpus or a paired test counts against it.
