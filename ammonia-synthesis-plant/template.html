Overall and synthesis loop balance model

The model is computed in Python. The precomputed default run is displayed on load; a live run replaces the displayed values. All rates are mass flow rates in kilogram per hour (kg/h, where kg denotes kilogram and h denotes hour). Part 1, the overall plant balance, takes the natural gas, process steam and process air feed rates and the process condensate, carbon dioxide product and purge gas rates, and returns the liquid ammonia product rate as the closing stream of the balance. Part 2, the synthesis loop balance, takes the fresh synthesis gas rate, the liquid ammonia product rate and the recycle gas rate, and returns the loop purge rate (fresh rate minus product rate), the converter feed rate (fresh rate plus recycle rate), the separator vapour rate (converter feed rate minus product rate), the recycle ratio (recycle rate divided by fresh rate, dimensionless) and the ammonia formation per pass (product rate divided by converter feed rate, in mass per cent).
