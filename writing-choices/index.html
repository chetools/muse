Writing choices

Each card shows two versions of the same text. They state the same facts and differ in one feature of the writing. Read the context line, then choose the version you consider better, or choose “No significant difference” where the difference does not matter to you. That answer is used: a feature with mostly “no significant difference” answers stays optional.

There are 38 cards. Two of them check that the questions are working; they are not identified. Four texts appear twice, in reverse order, as a check on the answers. Nothing is labelled with where either version came from.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you go. If you close the page, reopening this same link in the same browser resumes where you left off. At the end, a results screen gives a short coded summary: copy it and paste it into the chat, or download it as a file and attach the file.
