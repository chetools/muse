Synthesis loop and purification balance model

The model is computed in Python. The precomputed default run is displayed on load; a live run replaces the displayed values. All rates are mass flow rates in kilogram per hour (kg/h). Part 1, synthesis loop balance: the inputs are the fresh synthesis gas feed rate, the purge rate withdrawn from the separator vapour, and the circulation rate (the separator vapour returned to the converter inlet); the crude methanol rate is the closing product of the loop balance, and the outputs are the converter feed rate, the separator vapour rate, the recycle ratio (circulation divided by fresh feed, dimensionless) and the methanol formation per pass (crude methanol divided by converter feed, in mass per cent). Part 2, purification balance: the inputs are the light ends rate removed in the topping column T-401 and the aqueous bottoms rate of the refining column T-402, both model-level splits of the crude methanol; the refined methanol rate is the closing product, so the purification balance closes to the crude methanol feed exactly. The model definitions are stated in full in the Balance model section of the Theory panel.

Inputs differ from the displayed run. Press Run model to recompute.
