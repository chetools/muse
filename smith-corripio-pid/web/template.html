Smith and Corripio PID tuning

Tuning of a proportional-integral-derivative (PID) controller for a first-order plus dead time (FOPTD) model, starting from the Smith and Corripio integral of time-weighted absolute error (ITAE) correlations, with parameter sweeps, output limits, slew limiting and anti-windup. The page is written for chemical engineering students who have met Laplace transforms and the basics of feedback control. At the default model — process gain 1, time constant 10 s, dead time 2 s — the Smith and Corripio PID set-point tuning is a controller gain of 3.790, an integral time of 13.04 s and a derivative time of 0.691 s. Times are in seconds (s) and signals in % of span.

Metrics

Sweep

Sweeps start from the App tab tuning (Smith and Corripio by default) and use the App plant, limits, slew and anti-windup settings. Every run reports two criteria: the integral of absolute error (IAE, % s) and the integral of time-weighted absolute error (ITAE, % s²). The criterion selector in the main column chooses which one the slices, the heat map and the optimum display; IAE is the default. One-dimensional slices vary one of the controller gain Kc (dimensionless), the integral time Ti (s) and the derivative time Td (s) by factors from 0.25 to 4 (logarithmic, 25 points). Kc and Ti vary over the heat map (15 × 15), with Td held at the starting value.
