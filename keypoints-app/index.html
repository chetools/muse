Sign in and transcript sealing

For a graded take, sign in first: fill in the course server and the assignment code from your course page, enter your school email, and open the sign-in link that is emailed to you (on this device). Then record as usual. Sealing sends your audio — not the video — to the course server at the end of the take, as a single recording of the whole explanation. The server seals the audio and holds it: it cannot be changed afterwards, and nothing is transcribed on the server. Keep the sealed session code from your summary; the block on the end screen carries it to your Muse, which transcribes the sealed audio and compares your explanation against the standard for the assignment.
